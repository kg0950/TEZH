
# **TEZH - Traffic Energy Zenith Harvester**  
### **Sustainable Energy from Everyday Traffic**

### **Project Overview**  
The **Traffic Energy Zenith Harvester (TEZH)** is an innovative energy-generating speed breaker designed to harness the kinetic energy of moving vehicles and convert it into electrical power. TEZH integrates **piezoelectric elements** within speed breakers to generate electricity as vehicles pass over them, offering a sustainable and efficient method of energy harvesting in high-traffic areas.

### **How It Works**  
TEZH operates on the principle of piezoelectricity, where **piezoelectric materials** embedded within the speed breaker generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress. As vehicles roll over the speed breaker, the pressure exerted activates the piezo elements, which then convert this mechanical energy into electrical energy. This electricity can be stored in batteries or directly used to power streetlights, traffic signals, surveillance systems, or even fed into the local grid.

### **Key Features**  
- **Efficient Energy Harvesting** – Utilizes vehicle motion, an abundant and otherwise wasted source of kinetic energy.  
- **Eco-Friendly Solution** – Provides clean and renewable energy without additional fuel consumption or emissions.  
- **Durable and Low Maintenance** – Designed to withstand heavy traffic loads with minimal maintenance.  
- **Scalable Deployment** – Suitable for highways, city roads, parking lots, toll booths, and industrial zones.  
- **Smart Energy Storage** – Integrates with battery systems or smart grids for optimal energy utilization.  

### **Potential Applications**  
- Powering **streetlights** and **traffic signals** in urban and rural areas.  
- Supplying electricity to **CCTV cameras** for enhanced traffic monitoring and security.  
- Charging **EV stations** and other roadside infrastructure.  
- Contributing to **smart city initiatives** by enhancing sustainability.  

### **Impact and Future Prospects**  
TEZH aims to revolutionize road infrastructure by making traffic a **source of clean energy**. With increasing urbanization and vehicle usage, harnessing kinetic energy from traffic presents an **untapped opportunity** for sustainable power generation. By implementing TEZH in strategic locations, cities can **reduce their reliance on conventional energy sources**, lower carbon footprints, and move towards a greener future.
